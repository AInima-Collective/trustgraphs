## How this report becomes releasable

Follow [`runbook.md`](./runbook.md), retain immutable/redacted evidence outside the source tree, and
populate a copy of `rollout-evidence.example.json`. This command
is the mechanical minimum gate:

```sh
scripts/eas-offchain-soak-check.mjs /secure/evidence/eas-offchain-rollout.json
```

A passing command does not enable mainnet. It makes the evidence eligible for the separate
mainnet go/no-go decision required by the goal.
